Rui Zhao is a Research Executive Director, and the head of the Smart City R&D group and Financial Large Language Model group at [SenseTime](https://www.sensetime.com). Prior to this role, he was the CTO and Chief Scientist of SenseNets, an affiliated venture with SenseTime, during 2015–2017.

He received his Bachelor's degree from the [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](http://en.ustc.edu.cn/) in 2010, and his Ph.D. degree from [The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](http://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html) in 2015. At SenseTime, he leads pivotal research projects including face recognition and large language models in finance. His research interests span **multimodal large language models**, **deep learning**, and **computer vision**. He has accumulated over **10,000 Google Scholar citations** with more than **150 publications** over the last decade.

He is a recipient of the Shenzhen May 1 Labor Medal (2022), [Wu Wen Jun's AI Award for Scientific and Technology Progress](http://www.wuwenjunkejijiang.cn/wj/news.aspx?pkid=14026&tid=13390) (2019), Black Sheep Award from SenseTime (2018, 1‰ award rate for extraordinary contribution), Peacock Program Talent Award from Shenzhen Municipal Government (2016), Outstanding Reviewer Award from ECCV 2016, Doctoral Consortium Award from CVPR 2015, and Travel Award from ICCV 2013.

If you are looking to join SenseTime as an intern or full-time researcher, please [send your CV by email](mailto:zhaorui@sensetime.com). Candidates with a strong publication record and self-motivation are preferred.
